Step 1 • Match Leverage

Leverage Index ≥ 2.0

An XGBoost win-probability model simulates 6 outcomes (0, 1, 2–3, 4, 6+, W) before each ball. Deliveries with expected swing ≥ 2× the format-innings average are high-pressure.

Step 2 • Delivery Credit

WPA & Pressure Lift

Batters earn +WPA; bowlers earn -WPA (byes, leg-byes, penalties & run-outs excluded). Lift compares shrunken pressure rate against the player's LI < 2.0 baseline.

Step 3 • Final Score

Shrinkage & 75/25 PRI

Empirical-Bayes shrinkage pulls small/noisy samples toward the pool average (τ² ≥ 1.0). Final score: 50 + 10×[0.75·Z(shr) + 0.25·Z(lift)].

Key Limitations

  • Measures statistical match leverage, not psychological nerves.
  • 6-outcome LI simulation approximates ball transitions; special illegal-ball/free-hit rules are simplified.
  • Venue, pitch wear, weather, and exact batter-bowler matchups are not explicitly modeled.
  • First-innings model has lower discrimination (AUC 0.653) than the chase model (0.878).
