Step 1 • Pressure Definition

Match Leverage (LI ≥ 2.0)

Instead of labelling "overs 16–20" as pressure, an XGBoost Win Probability model evaluates 6 representative ball outcomes (0, 1, 2–3, 4, 6+, W).

Expected Swing = Σ [P(o) × |ΔWP(o)|]
LI = Expected Swing / Format-Innings Avg
Step 2 • Delivery Credit

Attributed WPA & Lift

Batters get +WPA on striker balls; bowlers get -WPA (including wides). Byes, leg-byes, penalties, and run-outs are excluded from individual credit.

Raw Rate = 100 × (ΣWPA / n)
Lift = Shrunken Rate − Low-Pressure Rate
Step 3 • Final Rating

Empirical-Bayes & PRI

Small or noisy samples are shrunk toward the pool average (≥30 balls, ≥3 matches; τ² ≥ 1.0), standardized via Z-scores, and combined 75/25.

λ = τ² / (τ² + s²/n)
PRI = 50 + 10 × [0.75·Z(shr) + 0.25·Z(lift)]

Delivery Attribution & Exclusions

  • Excluded extras: Byes, leg-byes, and penalty runs are excluded from individual batter/bowler WPA credit.
  • Non-bowler wickets: Run-outs and non-bowler dismissals do not grant individual batter/bowler WPA credit.
  • Wides: Excluded from striker ball/credit count, but remain attributed to the bowler's delivery WPA.
  • Bowler wickets: Descriptive wicket counts include only caught, bowled, LBW, stumped, and hit-wicket.
  • Elo & Normalization: Separate Elo ratings for IPL and T20I; LI denominator computed separately per format × innings.

Known Limitations & Next Tests

  • Measures match leverage, not psychological nerves or mental state.
  • 6-outcome simulation advances one ball in simplified states (does not capture every illegal-ball / free-hit rule transition).
  • Venue, pitch wear, weather, batter-bowler matchups, and field placements are not directly modeled.
  • Match-block bootstrap CIs and sensitivity re-ranks (LI ≥ 1.5 / 2.5, min balls 50 / 100, IPL-only vs T20I-only) remain future robustness checks.
